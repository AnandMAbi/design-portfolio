02 — About

Structure first.
Then the details.

I'm Anand, a visual designer working across mobile products, interface systems and brand identity. I like briefs that are a little messy at the start — where the problem is real, the constraints are honest, and the answer has to earn its place.

Most of the work here started as a rough idea on a call. I take it from there: structure first, then the interface, then the details that make it feel like nothing else.
